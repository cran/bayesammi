1 Abstract

Dozens of stability indices are in use, each computed once from the cell means of a trial. Computed on every posterior draw of the cell means instead, each index gets a posterior mean, an HPD interval and, for every genotype, a probability of being the best. bam_indices() does this for five classical indices and bam_ammi_indices() for six AMMI-based ones. The cell means come from any fit: AMMI or GGE (bayes_ammi()) or a stability model (bayes_stability()).

fit <-
  bayes_ammi(
      .data   = Maiz
    , .y      = y

    , .level  = 0.95
    , .seed   = 11
    , .align  = TRUE
    , .model  = "AMMI"
    , .het    = FALSE
    , .waic   = TRUE
    )

2 Classical indices

For one draw of the cell means \(\eta_{ij}\), with interaction \(\epsilon_{ij} = \eta_{ij} - \bar{\eta}_{i\cdot} - \bar{\eta}_{\cdot j} + \bar{\eta}_{\cdot\cdot}\):

Index Formula Better
Wricke’s ecovalence \(W_i = \sum_j \epsilon_{ij}^2\) smaller
Shukla’s stability variance \(\frac{G}{(G-2)(E-1)}W_i - \frac{\sum_k W_k}{(G-1)(G-2)(E-1)}\) smaller
Lin and Binns’ superiority \(\sum_j (\eta_{ij} - M_j)^2 / (2E)\), \(M_j\) the best in environment \(j\) smaller
Annicchiarico’s reliability \(\bar{Z}_i - z_{1-p} s_{Z_i}\), \(Z_{ij} = 100\,\eta_{ij}/\bar{\eta}_{\cdot j}\) larger
Eskridge’s safety-first \(\bar{\eta}_{i\cdot} - z_{1-\alpha} s_i\), \(s_i^2\) the variance across environments larger 
ind <-
  bam_indices(
      fit           = fit
    , higher_better = TRUE
    , .prob         = 0.25
    , .alpha        = 0.05
    )

The indices measure different things. Wricke’s and Shukla’s indices measure stability alone and rank genotypes in the same order. Lin and Binns’ index measures distance from the best, so it mixes mean and stability. The rank correlation between Wricke’s and Lin and Binns’ posterior means is 0.08. Shukla’s estimator can be negative, as in the classical analysis; 5 of its 9 HPD intervals reach below zero, a sign that the stability variance of those genotypes cannot be told from zero.

Because the indices are computed from the cell means, without plot error, they describe the stability of the expected performance. The HPD intervals show how well 20 environments determine them; they are wide.

3 AMMI-based indices

With coordinates \(\mathrm{PC}_{ik} = \lambda_k^{1/2}\alpha_{ik}\) and the share \(\theta_k = \lambda_k^2/\sum_m \lambda_m^2\) of each fitted term:

Index Formula Source
ASV \(\sqrt{(\lambda_1^2/\lambda_2^2\,\mathrm{PC}_{i1})^2 + \mathrm{PC}_{i2}^2}\) Purchase et al. (2000)
SIPC \(\sum_k \lvert\mathrm{PC}_{ik}\rvert\) Sneller et al. (1997)
EV \(\sum_k \alpha_{ik}^2 / K\) Zobel (1994)
Za \(\sum_k \theta_k \lvert\alpha_{ik}\rvert\) Zali et al. (2012)
WAAS \(\sum_k \theta_k \lvert\mathrm{PC}_{ik}\rvert\) Olivoto et al. (2019)
WAASY mean and WAAS rescaled to 0-100 and weighted Olivoto et al. (2019)

4 Indices from a stability model

The same classical indices can be computed from the cell means of a bayes_stability() fit:

fs <-
  bayes_stability(
      .data   = Maiz
    , .y      = y
    , .gen    = entry
    , .env    = site
    , .rep    = rep
    , .model  = "ER"
    , .nIter  = 2000
    , .burnin = 500
    , .thin   = 1
    , .chains = 1
    , .priors = bam_stab_priors(
          mu_var = NULL
        , g_var  = NULL
        , h_var  = NULL
        , b_var  = 100
        , nu     = 1
        , s2     = NULL
        , nu_d   = 1
        , s2_d   = NULL

    , .level  = 0.95
    , .alpha  = 0.05
    , .seed   = 11
    , .waic   = TRUE

5 Interpretation

bam_interpret(
    x             = ind
  , higher_better = TRUE
  , top           = 5
  )
Posterior stability indices

Best on average over the indices (mean expected rank): 6, 5, 3, 7 and 2.

Findings:
- Most probably the best on wricke: 7 (probability 0.45).
- Most probably the best on shukla: 7 (probability 0.45).
- Most probably the best on lin_binns: 6 (probability 0.71).
- Most probably the best on annicchiarico: 6 (probability 0.71).
- Most probably the best on safety_first: 6 (probability 0.54).
- The indices disagree on the best genotype: they weigh mean and stability
  differently.

Recommendations:
- Prefer genotypes that rank well on several indices and whose HPD intervals
  separate from the others; overlapping intervals mean the trial cannot tell
  them apart.

Cautions:
- Stability indices describe the environments of this trial; confirm over
  years before recommending.
