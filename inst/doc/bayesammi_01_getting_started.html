1 Abstract

bayesammi fits the AMMI model of a multi-environment trial by Bayesian methods (Crossa et al. 2011). This vignette goes from the plot data of the maize trial Maiz to posterior summaries, traces, the biplot with highest posterior density (HPD) regions and a written interpretation. Each later vignette takes one part further.

2 The data

Maiz holds the grain yield (kg/ha) of 9 maize genotypes in 20 sites, a randomized complete block design with 4 replicates in every site, one row per plot.

3 The least-squares AMMI fit

The classical AMMI analysis decomposes the interaction effects by their singular values. bayes_ammi() starts its chains there.

ls_fit <-
  ge_ammi(
      .data = Maiz
    , .y    = y
    , .gen  = entry
    , .env  = site
    , .rep  = rep
    )
round(ls_fit$lambdas)
[1] 5923 3070 2552 2320 1759 1313  793  757
ls_fit$sigma2
[1] 602844.9

4 The Bayesian fit

Two chains of 3000 kept iterations after a burn-in of 500 take a few seconds. The first two bilinear terms are fitted, as in Crossa et al. (2011).

fit <-
  bayes_ammi(
      .data   = Maiz
    , .y      = y
    , .gen    = entry
    , .env    = site
    , .rep    = rep
    , .nIter  = 3000
    , .k      = 2
    , .burnin = 500
    , .thin   = 1
    , .chains = 2
    , .priors = bam_priors(
          mu_mean     = 0
        , mu_var      = 1e+8
        , tau_mean    = 0
        , tau_var     = 1e+8
        , delta_mean  = 0
        , delta_var   = 1e+8
        , lambda_mean = 0
        , lambda_var  = 1e+8
        , nu          = 1
        , s2          = NULL
        )
    , .level  = 0.95
    , .seed   = 1
    , .align  = TRUE
    , .model  = "AMMI"
    , .het    = FALSE
    , .waic   = TRUE
    )

5 Traces and the biplot

plot(x = fit, which = "trace")
plot(x = fit, which = "biplot")

The biplot of the posterior means looks like a classical AMMI biplot. biplots() adds a region around every point that holds 95% (and 99%) of its posterior. A region that contains the origin marks a genotype or environment whose interaction is not different from zero.

bp <-
  biplots(
      model         = fit
    , burnin        = 0
    , thin          = 1
    , pb            = c(0.05, 0.01)
    , plot_stable   = TRUE
    , plot_unstable = TRUE
    , ncolors       = 5
    , .which        = "both"
    , .grid         = 100
    )

6 Interpretation

bam_interpret(
    x             = fit
  , higher_better = TRUE
  , top           = 5
  )
Bayesian AMMI analysis

2 bilinear term(s): lambda_1 = 5,459, lambda_2 = 1,695; the chains have
converged.

Findings:
- The chains have converged by the criteria of Crossa et al. (2011) (largest
  potential scale reduction factor 1.004); the smallest effective sample size
  of the main parameters is 657.
- 4 of 132 Heidelberger-Welch stationarity and half-width tests failed (about
  5% fail by chance at level 0.05).
- Overall mean 4,858 (95% HPD 4,796 to 4,920); error variance 762,200, a
  coefficient of variation of 18.0%.
- Genotypes significantly above average (HPD of the effect excludes zero): 4,
  5 and 6; below average: 1, 2, 7 and 8.
- Environments with significantly high means: 4, 6, 11, 12, 13 and 2 more;
  low: 1, 2, 7, 8, 10 and 2 more.
- Singular values with 95% HPD intervals: lambda_1 = 5,459 (4,527 to 6,306);
  lambda_2 = 1,695 (59.23 to 2,892).
- On the first bilinear term, genotypes with significantly positive scores:
  4, 5, 6 and 9; negative: 1, 2, 3 and 8.
- Environments with significantly positive first scores: 11, 12, 13, 15 and
  18; negative: 1, 3, 8 and 10.
- Genotypes and environments with significant first scores of the same sign
  interact positively: the genotype yields more there than its main effect
  predicts; of opposite sign, less.

Recommendations:
- Recommend the genotypes with significant first scores for the environments
  whose scores have the same sign (biplots() shows the regions).
- Draw biplots() to see which genotypes and environments form homogeneous
  groups (overlapping HPD regions).

Cautions:
- The HPD interval of lambda_2 reaches down to near zero: the term is weakly
  supported and its scores are uncertain.
